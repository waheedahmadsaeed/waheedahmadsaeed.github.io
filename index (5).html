MECH3425 · Automotive Propulsion Systems

Diesel Engine Practical — Energy Balance & AFR Effects

Operated a diesel engine at a steady condition (~2200 rpm, full load) to produce a quantified energy balance diagram, identifying the split between brake power output, exhaust sensible heat and cooling losses. Recorded cylinder pressure data and produced a P-V diagram, comparing the real cycle against the ideal diesel cycle and explaining deviations due to heat transfer, combustion timing and pumping losses. Varied fuelling at constant speed to assess the effect of air-fuel ratio on torque, brake power, brake thermal efficiency and specific fuel consumption.

Energy balance at full load (2200 rpm) P-V diagram vs ideal diesel cycle AFR effects on BTE and BSFC

MECH3900 · Finite Element Methods · Group Work · CW2

Composite Layup Optimisation — Robinson R22 Horizontal Stabiliser

Applied carbon-epoxy composite material to the horizontal stabiliser of a Robinson R22 rotorcraft using Abaqus FEA. Validated single-ply and multi-ply shell models against MATLAB and hand calculations across fibre orientations (0°, 90°, 253°), then systematically tested over 20 layup configurations across 1, 6, 8, 10 and 12-ply setups to optimise weight reduction while maintaining structural integrity versus the aluminium baseline (2024-T3). Mesh-independent results confirmed at 5mm element size across convergence studies from 25mm down to 2.5mm.

21.9% weight reduction vs aluminium baseline 673.1g → 525.91g optimal composite Optimal layup: [0, 0, 45, −45, 90, 90, −45, 45, 0, 0] FE validated against MATLAB & hand calcs Mesh-independent at 5mm element size
